Our Story

Your Complete ICT Solutions Partner

Single Cloud TecHub started with a simple idea: Kenyan schools, churches, hospitals, property owners and retailers deserve software built for how they actually operate — not generic tools forced to fit. We design, build, deploy and host complete systems, from the first line of code to the server it runs on.

Today that means everything from M-Pesa-enabled POS systems and multi-tenant Church ERPs to CBC-ready school management platforms, hospital billing systems, and the hardware, networking and support that keep it all running. Every system is built, deployed and supported end to end, right here in Kenya.
